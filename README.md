# zara_sales_analysis_EDA
This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Zara sales data to understand customer buying behavior, product performance, pricing strategy, promotion effectiveness, and seasonal trends.   The main goal of this analysis is to generate business insights that can help improve sales performance and marketing decisions. 
Objectives 

Analyze top-selling products  

Understand the impact of promotions on sales  

Compare category-wise and brand-wise performance  

Analyze seasonal vs non-seasonal products  

Study pricing impact on sales volume  

Identify how product placement affects sales  

Generate business recommendations from data 

Dataset Information 

The dataset contains product-related sales information such as: 

Product ID  

Product Position  

Promotion Status  

Product Category  

Seasonal Information  

Sales Volume  

Brand  

Product Name & Description  

Price  

Currency  

Store Section  

Tools & Libraries Used 

Python  

Pandas  

NumPy  

Matplotlib  

Seaborn  

Jupyter Notebook 

Data Cleaning 

The following preprocessing steps were performed: 

Checked missing values  

Removed duplicates  

Handled null values  

Created new calculated columns  

Converted data into analysis-friendly format  

 
 
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) 

1. Top Selling Products 

Analyzed products with the highest sales volume. 
 
 
Insight 

Key Insights 

Products like PLAID TIE-DYE OVERSHIRT, SUIT JACKET IN 100% LINEN, and RIB COLLAR JACKET are among the highest-selling items. 

Jacket and overshirt products dominate customer demand, showing strong preference for outerwear and fashion essentials. 

Sales volume among top products is very close, meaning multiple products are consistently performing well instead of relying on only one bestseller. 

 

Business Impact 

1. Inventory Planning 

These products should always remain in stock. Stock-outs on these items may directly reduce revenue opportunities. 

Recommendation: 

Maintain higher inventory levels for top-performing products  

Monitor stock availability weekly  

 

2. Marketing Focus 

Best-selling products can be used in: 

Homepage banners  

Social media ads  

Email campaigns  

Seasonal promotions  

Recommendation: 

Prioritize these products in promotional campaigns.  

Highlight them as “Best Sellers” on website/store  

 

3. Product Strategy 

Customers show a strong interest in jackets and premium fashion wear. 

Recommendation: 

Expand similar product styles and collections  

Launch related variants (colors, fits, seasonal editions)  

 

4. Revenue Optimization 

Since these products already have strong demand, even small pricing optimization can improve overall revenue. 

Recommendation: 

Test slight premium pricing on high-demand products  

Bundle top-selling products with accessories. 

2. Section-wise Sales Analysis 
 
 

Key Insights 

The MAN section dominates overall sales volume compared to the WOMAN section. Sales from the MAN category are significantly higher, showing stronger customer demand and better product performance. The WOMAN section contributes a much smaller share of total sales, indicating potential growth opportunities. 
 

Business Impact 

1. Strong Demand in Men’s Fashion 

Men’s products are currently the main revenue-driving segment. 

Recommendation: 

Continue expanding high-performing men’s collections  

Increase inventory for best-selling men’s products  

Launch more variations of successful items  

 

2. Improvement Opportunity in Women’s Section 

Lower sales in the WOMAN section may indicate the following: 

Limited product variety  

Pricing mismatch  

Weak promotions  

Less attractive product positioning  

Recommendation: 

Improve women’s product assortment  

Introduce trend-based collections  

Run targeted promotions for women customers.  

 

3. Marketing Strategy Optimization 

The business can use MEN products for stable revenue while improving WOMAN category growth. 

Recommendation: 

Create separate marketing campaigns for WOMAN section  

Use influencers/social media campaigns targeting female audience  

Promote seasonal women's collections more aggressively 

 
 

 

 

 

 
 

3. Promotion Impact Analysis 
 
 

Key Insights 

Promoted products account for slightly higher sales performance compared to non-promoted products. Around 51.3% of sales are influenced by promotional activities, while 48.7% come from non-promoted products. The difference is not extremely large, which shows that Zara products already have strong organic demand even without heavy discounts. 

 

Business Impact 

1. Promotions Positively Influence Sales 

Promotional campaigns help increase customer purchases and product visibility. 

Recommendation: 

Continue running smart promotional campaigns  

Use promotions during seasonal launches and clearance periods  

 

2. Strong Brand Value 

Nearly half of the sales come without promotions, showing strong brand loyalty and customer trust. 

Recommendation: 

Maintain premium brand positioning  

Avoid excessive discounting that may reduce perceived brand value  

 

3. Opportunity for Strategic Promotions 

Promotions should focus on: 

Medium-performing products  

Seasonal inventory  

New product launches  

Recommendation: 

Use limited time offers  

Bundle products to increase average order value  

Promote slow-moving inventory strategically 

 

4. Seasonal Product Analysis 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Key Insights 

Seasonal and non-seasonal products show very similar sales performance. Seasonal products have a slightly higher sales volume, indicating that season-based demand positively influences customer purchases. The small difference suggests that Zara maintains stable demand throughout the year, not only during seasonal periods. 

 

Business Impact 

1. Seasonal Products Still Matter 

Seasonal collections help boost customer engagement and trend-based purchases. 

Recommendation: 

Launch seasonal collections before peak demand periods  

Focus on trend-driven seasonal marketing campaigns  

 

2. Stable Non-Seasonal Demand 

Non-seasonal products continue generating strong sales consistently. 

Recommendation: 

Maintain core evergreen products throughout the year  

Keep best-selling essentials permanently available  

 

3. Balanced Product Portfolio 

Zara benefits from having both: 

Trend-driven seasonal products  

Stable year-round products  

Recommendation: 

Balance inventory between seasonal and evergreen collections  

Use seasonal launches to attract traffic while evergreen products maintain consistent revenue 

 

 

5. Price vs Sales Relationship 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Key Insights 

Most products are concentrated in the low to medium price range, showing Zara’s strong focus on affordable fashion. Lower-priced products generally achieve higher sales volume because they are accessible to a larger customer base. Some high-priced products still generate strong sales, indicating that premium products also have demand among customers. Extremely expensive products have lower sales volume, which is expected due to a smaller target audience. 

 

Business Impact 

1. Affordable Products Drive Customer Traffic 

Mid-range and affordable products are the main contributors to sales volume. 

Recommendation: 

Continue offering competitively priced fashion products  

Use affordable products to attract more customers  

 

2. Premium Products Increase Revenue 

High-priced products may sell less frequently but can generate strong profit margins. 

Recommendation: 

Promote premium collections strategically  

Highlight premium products in featured sections and campaigns  

 

3. Balanced Pricing Strategy is important. 

The business benefits from maintaining both: 

High-volume affordable products  

High-margin premium products  

Recommendation: 

Maintain a balanced product portfolio  

Use affordable items for traffic generation and premium items for revenue growth 

 

 

7. Product Position Analysis 
 

Key Insights 

Products placed at the front of the store generated the highest average sales volume. Aisle placements also performed strongly, showing that product visibility plays a major role in customer buying behavior. End-cap positions generated slightly lower sales compared to other positions but still maintained stable performance. 

 

Business Impact 

1. Store Visibility Directly Impacts Sales 

Products placed in high-visibility areas attract more customer attention and increase purchase chances. 

Recommendation: 

Place best-selling and premium products at the Front of Store  

Use eye-catching displays for featured products  

 

2. Strategic Product Placement Can Increase Revenue 

Proper store layout can improve customer engagement and product discovery. 

Recommendation: 

Rotate promotional and seasonal products into high-traffic locations  

Use aisle displays for trending items  

 

3. Optimize Underperforming Areas 

End-cap placement shows slightly lower performance compared to Front of Store. 

Recommendation: 

Improve end-cap visual merchandising  

Add promotional signage or discounts to boost attention
